# Block 147 — Archival Photo Sourcing Guide

> Companion to `Block147_HUMS_EN.pptx`. The deck already ships with the Pervititch raster, the block zoom, the church photo, the NW corner crop, and the flow diagram (in `output/presentation/`). **This guide is for the *additional* historical / archival photos you'll want to drop in by hand** — they sharpen the demographic-and-physical-transformation story without depending on copyrighted brand sites.

For every photo: target slide, exact placement coordinates in inches (the deck is 13.333 × 7.5 inches), what to search for, the recommended public-domain or CC source, and a caption / attribution template.

## Copyright cheat-sheet (one paragraph)

For a single academic / non-commercial presentation in Türkiye and the EU, you can safely use: **(a)** anything on **Wikimedia Commons CC-BY-SA** with attribution in the caption; **(b)** anything from **Library of Congress / Photochrom** which is **public domain**; **(c)** anything on **SALT Research** that displays a "Açık Kaynak / Open Source" badge — others require permission form `arsiv@saltonline.org`; **(d)** anything from **İBB Atatürk Kitaplığı** for educational use, with citation; **(e)** **brand photos** from the brand's own website (Beyaz Fırın, Akveren, Mehmet Efendi), citing the source URL. For the Kaloumenos pogrom photographs, cite the Ecumenical Patriarchate as the institutional holder. For anything else (especially Ara Güler or named contemporary photographers), you must obtain explicit permission — substitute with your own photo or skip.
