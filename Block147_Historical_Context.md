## 2. Ayia Efimia church — history (useful for the church model)

- Byzantine-era foundation, fell into ruin.
- **Rebuilt 1694** under the permission obtained by **Gabriel, Metropolitan of Kadıköy (Chalcedon)**.
- **Enlarged / rebuilt to its present form in 1830** by **Metropolitan Zacharias II**, financed by donations collected in Russia.
- **Form:** rectangular plan, **brick** structure, **tiled roof**, with a **central high-drum dome carried on four arches resting on four massive pier ("elephant-leg") pilasters**.
- Kadıköy was the seat of the **Metropolis of Chalcedon**; the church served the substantial Kadıköy Rum (Greek Orthodox) community.
- Neglected through the 20th c.; restored and re-opened for worship **1 April 1993** under Metropolitan Iokeim III.

#### Sokak adı göçü (haritayı doğru okumanın anahtarı)
- Pervititch'in **"Söğütlü Çeşme Cad."** etiketi (Pafta 147'nin BATI kenarı) → bugünkü **Yasa Caddesi**
- Pervititch'in **"T…L Sok."** etiketi (Pafta 147'nin KUZEY kenarı) → bugünkü **Söğütlüçeşme Caddesi**
- (Söğütlü çeşmenin adı, batıdaki söğüt-çeşmenin bulunduğu sokaktan, çevredeki bir başka sokağa göç etmiş. Çeşme — parsel (39/2) — hâlâ yerinde.)

#### NW köşesi: tek bir Pervititch `FİRİN` kompleksinin bugünkü üç sahibi
- Pervititch'in 1923/1938 sayfasında **(40)** üzerinde `FİRİN` ve **(34)/(36)** üzerinde `Fırın` aynı, **köşeyi saran bir fırın tesisini** çiziyor (v3 register de bunu `(40)/(98)-cplx` olarak ele aldı).
- Bugün bu tek tesis **iki cepheye ve üç kullanıcıya** bölünmüş durumda:
  - BATI cephesi (Yasa Cd.) → Beyaz Fırın (1920+)
  - KUZEY cephesi (Söğütlüçeşme Cd.) → Akveren (1925+) ve Mehmet Efendi şubesi (sonradan)
- 1938 zaman çakışması: Beyaz Fırın 18 yaşında, Akveren 13 yaşında, ikisi de aynı köşede çalışıyor; Pervititch hepsini `Fırın` *bina tipi* etiketi altında topluyor (marka adı yazmıyor — sigorta haritasının amacı yangın riski, marka değil).

> **Caveat (dürüst not):** "Söğütlüçeşme Cd. 12 = Pervititch parselinin tam olarak hangisi?" sorusunu kamuya açık bir kaynak kesin söyleyemiyor; bunun için tapu-kadastro örtüşmesi gerek. Koordinat eşleşmeleri ~5–10 m hassasiyetle **üç markayı da Pafta 147'nin NW köşesine** koyuyor — sunum için sağlam dayanak bu.

> **The block's *mixed fabric* in one sentence:**
> **Rum Ortodoks** kilisesi (1694/1830) + **Bulgar Ortodoks** fırını (Beyaz Fırın, 1920) + **Türk-Müslüman** makarnacı (Akveren, 1925) + **Türk-Müslüman** kuru-kahveci şubesi (Mehmet Efendi, 1871/sonra) + Ermeni eczacı + Rum şekerci + Osmanlı çeşmesi — **bir 1372 m²'lik ada, en az 100 yıllık kesintisiz bir gıda-ve-mâbet omurgası, yedi ayrı kimlik.**

### ★ The Fırın on Block 147 — original write-up (superseded by the table above)

The bakery drawn at the **NW corner of Block 147** — `FİRİN` on the north-frontage parcel **(40)** and `Fırın` along the Söğütlü Çeşme Cd. frontage **(34)/(36)** — is, in all likelihood, **Beyaz Fırın**, the historic Kadıköy bakery/*pastane* that is still trading on the same corner today.

- **Family line:** **Kosma (Kozma) Stoyanof / Stanyof**, a Bulgarian-Macedonian Orthodox immigrant, opened a *simit* bakery in **Balat in 1836**.
- **Kadıköy founding:** his son **Dimitri Stoyanof** (*"Dimitri Stanyof, of Üsküdar"*) **settled in Kadıköy and opened Beyaz Fırın in 1920** — still run by the family (5th generation).
- **Location:** **Osmanağa Mahallesi, Yasa Caddesi No. 23, Kadıköy**; Cultural Inventory coordinates **40.9908066° N, 29.0248280° E** — which falls exactly at the **NW corner / west edge of Pervititch Block 147** (block centroid ≈ 40.9907° N, 29.0251° E), i.e. precisely where the sheet draws the `FİRİN` / `Fırın` block.
- **Corroboration:** a Beyaz Fırın write-up notes that **a 1938 Kadıköy insurance map (the Pervititch-type survey) shows a bakery exactly where Beyaz Fırın now stands** — and the building codes on the sheet (`MB` brick walls, `V.F.` concrete vault, `2½` storeys, `TR.4` Turkish-tile roof zone) read as the substantial masonry bakery a *fırın* needs.
- **Timeline check:** Beyaz Fırın opened in Kadıköy **1920**; the Pervititch Kadıköy survey was made **~1922–24** (updated **1938**) — so the `FİRİN` on the sheet is a then-2-to-18-year-old Beyaz Fırın. ✔

**Caveat:** the exact mapping of *which* Pervititch parcel number equals today's "Yasa Cd. No. 23" can't be fixed without overlaying the modern address on the historic cadastre (the streets were renamed — "İsmail Sok." / "Söğütlü Çeşme Cd." → "Yasa Cd." / "Mühürdar Cd."). But the coordinate match + the 1938-map note + the 1920 founding date make the identification a safe one for the presentation: **the bakery on Pervititch Block 147 is Beyaz Fırın, founded in Kadıköy by Dimitri Stoyanof in 1920.**

> *Note:* Beyaz Fırın's founders were **Bulgarian-Macedonian Orthodox**, while Block 147's church is **Greek (Rum) Orthodox** — two different Orthodox communities sharing the same Kadıköy Çarşı quarter, a nice illustration of the block's mixed fabric (Rum church, Bulgarian bakery, Armenian/Greek pharmacists & confectioners, Ottoman fountain).

## 4. The Annuaire Oriental — what it can and can't give us

- **The 1922 Annuaire Oriental does *not* cover Kadıköy.** Its "Adresses de Constantinople" section explicitly lists only the quarters **Stamboul · Galata · Péra · Pancaldi (Pangaltı) · Férikeuy (Feriköy)** — the Asian side and the outer suburbs were cut from the slimmed-down 1921–22 (Allied-occupation-era) editions.
- **The 1914 Annuaire Oriental (SALT scan, ~1,900 pp.) — checked page-by-page — *also* has no Kadıköy "rues" (by-street) section.** Its Constantinople street directory runs ABANOS…ZUMBUL with every street qualified *Stamboul / Galata / Péra / Pancaldi / Grand-Bazar* — European side only, exactly like the 1922. The Table des Matières has **no "Kadi-Keuy" entry** in 1914 or 1922.
- **No Annuaire edition gives a parcel-by-parcel "who's at parcel N of Block 147" listing** for a suburb — that data structure does not exist in it, and the Annuaire's house numbers do **not** correspond to Pervititch parcel numbers. In the bigger editions Kadıköy people appear only (a) scattered through the alphabetical-by-name list with addresses like *"…, à Kadikeuy"* / *"R. Sutlu Tchechmé 73, à Kadikeuy"*, or (b) in a small **"Kadi-Keuy" trades sub-section** (esnaf grouped by profession), if present that year.

- Other named Kadıköy professionals in 1889-90 (street not specified): pharmacists **Nig Batmazian**, **Armand Ergan**; physicians **Dr Jean Théodoridi**, **Dr L. Vitalis**
- Kadıköy administration (1889-90): *Commissaire de police* = **Servet Bey**; *chef des pompiers* = **İsmaïl Effendi**; Kadıköy = the **10th municipal circle** of Constantinople
- Religious (1889-90): **Greek Orthodox Ecumenical Patriarchate** under Patriarch **Dionysios V** (the **Metropolis of Chalcedon / Kadıköy** falls under it); Catholic parish of Chalcedon = *L'Assomption de la Vierge*, curé **Mgr Joseph de Negri**, + the Tubini chapel at Kadıköy
- *(All other 1889-90 Kadıköy names are tied to Moda / Kuşdili / Bahariye / Mühürdar / Couche-Dili — not this block.)*
